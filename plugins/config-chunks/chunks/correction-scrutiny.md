## Correction Scrutiny

A **correction is a claim**, not cleanup. It must clear the same evidence bar as
the claim it overturns — a higher one, since it arrives wearing the authority of
a fix.

- **Cite the check that falsified the original claim.** Not "this is wrong" — the
  command you ran, the line you read, the counterexample you exhibited, grounded
  in the live artifact rather than the stale narration around it. A correction
  with no citation is an opinion overwriting a record.
- **A correction you can't falsify-check, you don't make.** Downgrade it to a
  flagged doubt — "unverified, worth checking" — and leave the original standing.
  A doubt costs one line; a wrong correction deletes a right answer.
- **Watch the framing that suppresses the check.** "I'm fixing an error" quietly
  answers "am I sure it *is* an error?" Generic "verify your claims" guidance
  never fires here, because the corrector believes they **are** the verification.
- **Corrections propagate; original claims sit still.** A wrong claim lives in one
  place; a correction gets copied downstream as a standing warning. Weight the
  check by how far it will travel.

This is an evidence bar, not a tone rule. It raises the cost of *issuing* a
correction — yielding fewer and better ones, not more hedging or self-criticism.

The failure mode this prevents: a confident, uncited "actually, that's wrong"
erasing a correct finding, then riding downstream as doctrine for weeks.
